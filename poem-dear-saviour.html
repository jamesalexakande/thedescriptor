Dear Saviour

Dear Saviour,
Why dost thou dim your light, O
Wayfinder - the shining versions of yourself,
You continually cause to shrink.
You embrace rooms with the courage of a cowardly mouse
Shrieking from action at the demands of the whips
Of the fears of perfection

They chatter about in your mind, disembodied voices gasping for air
Seeking the most precious thing in all the eorld-
Your gaze. They know more of you than you may ever know,
Wayfinder - a name you constantly forget
As you lose pieces of yourself.
You pick up the shattered pieces on the gemstone'd plains of your heart,
Whispering - Is anyone home? you find yourself
On the shores of your own faultlines, the sand on your beaches
Extending into the ocean, the familiar song of uncharted waters
fading into echoes of the pieces you gave away,

O wayfinder, when will you wake and see?
That the thing that thou seekers
Dost seeks thee -
If only you
Let your light...
Breathe.
