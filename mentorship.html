I lead a small number of courses and mentorship sessions each year — some online, some in physical locations. Below is what's currently running, along with a space to register your interest.

Course

The Universal Power of Story

Format & location details available on request

There are few things that do not distinguish between race, as the concept of story. Story does not discriminate. Story cuts through gender, race, tribe, or tongue. Stories are how the great lessons of the world are preserved — they show that everyone, regardless of skin colour, carries a shared humanity, and an ability to be touched and moved to action by the power of narrative.

In this session, you'll explore the sacredness and power of stories, and how to weave, prioritize, and elevate them in your writing — whether fiction or non-fiction.
