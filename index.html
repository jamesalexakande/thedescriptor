About

James Akande

James doesn't write to find out what he already believes. He writes to chase wisdom, whimsy, and wonder in the seemingly ordinary and mundane: the small, overlooked moments most of us walk straight past. This has in part taken shape as BathroomThoughts, a Saturday-morning essay built on one conviction: the world is full of wonderful moments, and unexpected wisdom lies around every corner.

He also explores art and sound interpretation — reading meaning into images and sound the way he reads meaning into an ordinary afternoon.

His work moves between poetry, creative non-fiction, and the occasional short story, circling questions of faith, identity, and the quiet architecture of everyday life — the waiting rooms, the small mercies, the things we almost miss. He is the author of Tales of a Shepherd: Musings on Psalm 23, a collection of reflective essays walking verse by verse through one of scripture's most quoted psalms, and The Infinite Now, a poetry collection built around presence and what it costs to actually stay in a moment.

Alongside his own writing, James mentors other writers — leading sessions like The Universal Power of Story, Poetry 101, and Exploring the Craft (co-led with author Felicity Joseph) — and is slowly building toward a small publishing house for writers doing quiet, honest, spiritually attentive work. He's also building The Vantage and its community expression, the Genesis Hub — with something new called The Forge quietly taking shape behind them.
