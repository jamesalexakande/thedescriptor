Demeter

You are...Demeter.
Goddess of earth, spring and soil,
Radiant life on very earth,
The forests worship your every step
You are... Demeter:
Goddess. Radiant. Wise.

You make your home amidst the
creatures of the wild; surrounded
by the spirits of wind and tree.
Nature is itself, your open field,
your childish playground,
your regal seat;

In blissful harmony. And for an instant
your features become free - tossing
all caution to the wind until
you pick them back up again -
drifting pieces beneath shifting
faces - of life; for when the satyrs go,
and the creatures give way to the night,
you will be true - once again;

O Demeter. The earth rumbles
beneath your steps. Zeus and the others
be damned, you have burdens yours
to bear; thoughts that seep beneath
your vivifying gaze. Radiant light
giving way to shades only Selene sees -

Secrets yours alone to keep. Kaleidoscopes
with reflective lights, hints at broken parts pouring out?
Perhaps. Magic is a skill you use, brightest
in the dark, as Cyclones within your tides
keep themselves at bay beneath
the waters of your ocean deep;

You are the sea. Strong enough,
You sweep many beneath your waves;
Yet the depths of your chasms, none has
found. For if they saw what lurked beneath;
Resplendent radiance, your brilliant light,
Beyond hidden monsters, and depths of dark,
would they run in terror, or gasp at light?
Perhaps. You are the sea -
churning,
bursting...
holding its secrets safely beneath.
