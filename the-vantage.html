The Vantage

A platform and a community for those who create from perspective.

The Vantage is both a platform and a community where creatives are introduced to the concept of creating from perspectives beyond the jaundice of the dual tree of Good and Evil that so often plagues our modern world, and by extension, that which we create.

Why It Exists

Heart craft before output

Because we believe that the heart of the creator is more important than what is created, we prioritize heart craft and redemptive frameworks — exploring what it means to architect solutions across different industries and spheres of life that shine unbiasedly and usher in Kingdom impositions, leading to creations beyond side effects.

Who It's For

If you create, innovate, or architect — anything

Writer or cook, startup founder or artist: if what you do brings something new into the world, The Vantage is for you. This is a space for creators across every field who want their work to carry more than technique — to carry perspective.
