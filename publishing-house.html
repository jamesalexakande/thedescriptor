Publishing House

An imprint for a particular kind of writer.

Somewhere down the road, I want to build a small publishing house — one that exists for writers whose work sits at the intersection of the sacred, the honest, and the beautifully unpolished. This page is the early, public sketch of that idea.

The Vision

Why start one at all?

Publishing, at its best, is an act of hospitality — someone deciding that your voice deserves a home. I've been on the receiving end of that hospitality, and I'd like to eventually extend it to others: writers doing quiet, reflective, spiritually honest work that doesn't always fit neatly into the mainstream.

Who It's For

The kind of writer in mind

Poets, essayists, and memoirists writing from a place of faith, reflection, and lived experience — work that values craft as much as it values honesty. Details on genre, format, and submission process will be shared here as the imprint takes shape.

Follow the process

This is very early. If you'd like to hear how it develops — and be first in line when it opens — the newsletter is the best place to follow along.
